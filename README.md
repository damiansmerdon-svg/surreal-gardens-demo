# Mushroom Walk

A phone demo of the Surreal Gardens AR mushroom stop at the First Creek Wetland, Adelaide Botanic Garden: an intro, a map from the Goodman Building, and the fungus forest growing beside the stepping stones, over a 360 photo of the spot or over the phone's live camera.

Open it on a phone in Safari or Chrome. Live camera mode needs the page served over HTTPS (GitHub Pages) and permission for the camera and motion sensors.

Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL).
