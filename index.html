Mushroom Walk

A short walk from the Goodman Building to the stepping stones in the First Creek Wetland. When you get there, hold up your phone: fungi push up out of the ground beside the stones, giants rise and lean over you, and spores drift in the light.

About 150 m, two minutes on foot. Real and surreal, never cute: they grow, open, shed, tear and sink back into the ground.

Finding where you are…

Leave the Goodman Building by the garden side, follow the edge of the Kitchen Garden, and step onto the stones into the wetland. Stop where the marker is.
